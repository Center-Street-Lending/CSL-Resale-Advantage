1. Affiliated Business Arrangement (AfBA) Disclosure & Common Ownership Notice

MANDATORY REGULATORY DISCLOSURE • RESPA & CALIFORNIA DRE COMPLIANCE

This Notice is provided pursuant to Section 8 of the Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act (RESPA, 12 U.S.C. § 2607, 12 C.F.R. § 1024.15) and California Business & Professions Code § 10177.4. Center Street Lending Corp., Innovate Realty Inc., and Honest Escrow Inc. are affiliated business entities operating under common ownership and control.

1.1 Nature of the Affiliation & "Common Umbrella"

You are hereby notified that Center Street Lending Corp. ("CSL"), Innovate Realty Inc. ("Innovate"), and Honest Escrow Inc. ("Honest") share common corporate parentage, common management, and/or overlapping beneficial ownership (the "Common Umbrella").

Because of this affiliated relationship, referrals of settlement, brokerage, or lending business among these entities may provide a financial or other commercial benefit to the common ownership group, the respective corporate entities, and their officers or directors. Specifically:

  • Center Street Lending Corp. may introduce or refer its borrowers to Innovate Realty Inc. for real estate listing, marketing, and brokerage services.
  • Center Street Lending Corp. and/or Innovate Realty Inc. may introduce or refer buyers or sellers to Honest Escrow Inc. for escrow, settlement, and closing coordination.
  • Honest Escrow Inc. maintains synchronized closing workflows with Center Street Lending Corp. to expedite payoff statements and satisfaction documentation.

1.2 Complete Freedom of Choice (No Required Use)

YOU ARE NOT REQUIRED TO USE INNOVATE REALTY INC. OR HONEST ESCROW INC. AS A CONDITION OF OBTAINING, MAINTAINING, EXTENDING, MODIFYING, OR PAYING OFF A LOAN WITH CENTER STREET LENDING CORP.

THERE ARE FREQUENTLY OTHER SETTLEMENT SERVICE PROVIDERS AVAILABLE WITH SIMILAR SERVICES. YOU ARE FREE TO SHOP AROUND TO DETERMINE THAT YOU ARE RECEIVING THE BEST SERVICES AND THE BEST RATE FOR THESE SERVICES.

Participation in the CSL Resale Advantage program is 100% voluntary, optional, and structured strictly à la carte:

  • Listing Only: You may list your property with Innovate Realty at the 1% listing commission while using any licensed escrow or title company of your choice.
  • Escrow Only: You may utilize Honest Escrow for the $500 flat seller-side escrow fee while listing your property with any independent broker of your choice, or while selling the property as a For-Sale-By-Owner (FSBO).
  • Both Services: You may combine Innovate Realty and Honest Escrow to achieve maximum workflow alignment and fee savings.
  • Neither Service: You may choose to use entirely separate brokers and escrow companies with zero penalty or adverse impact on your current or future lending relationships with Center Street Lending Corp.

2. CSL Resale Advantage Program Terms & Conditions

2.1 Purpose & Program Scope

The CSL Resale Advantage Program is a preferred client resource designed exclusively for real estate investors and borrowers financing California investment, commercial spec-construction, bridge, or fix-and-flip properties through Center Street Lending Corp.

2.2 Eligibility Requirements

To qualify for the discounted commission rates and escrow fee caps under the Program:

  1. The participant must be an active Center Street Lending borrower in good standing, or an approved alumna/alumnus borrower with a verified history of satisfactory payoff.
  2. The real property must be located within the State of California and held strictly for commercial, business, or investment purposes. Consumer, owner-occupied residential properties are strictly ineligible.
  3. The participant must sign a standard Exclusive Right to Sell Listing Agreement with Innovate Realty Inc. and/or formal Escrow Instructions with Honest Escrow Inc.

2.3 1% Listing Commission Terms (Innovate Realty Inc.)

  • Listing Agent Representation: The 1% commission compensates Innovate Realty Inc. for marketing and managing the listing side of the transaction.
  • Buyer's Broker Commission Discretion: The seller retains complete discretion over whether to offer, and what amount to offer, to a cooperating buyer's broker. Buyer broker compensation is not included in the 1% fee.
  • InnoLaunch Suite: Access to complimentary pre-completion marketing, digital campaigns, photography, and syndication is contingent upon executing an active exclusive listing agreement with an Innovate Realty agent.

2.4 $500 Flat Seller-Side Escrow Terms (Honest Escrow Inc.)

  • Covered Fees: The $500 cap applies strictly to Honest Escrow's base seller closing fee for 1–4 unit residential investment properties.
  • Excluded Third-Party Costs: The flat fee does not cover title insurance premiums (CLTA/ALTA), documentary transfer taxes, county recording fees, wire fees, courier fees, HOA certification fees, or lender payoff statement charges.
  • Neutral Third-Party Fiduciary: Honest Escrow acts solely as a neutral escrow depository pursuant to California Financial Code § 17000 et seq., and does not act as an attorney, financial advisor, or real estate broker for either party.

2.5 Right to Modify or Terminate

Center Street Lending, Innovate Realty, and Honest Escrow reserve the right to amend, suspend, or terminate this program at any time. Any changes will not affect transactions with fully executed listing agreements or opened escrow instructions prior to the date of modification.

3. Comprehensive Privacy Policy (CCPA / CPRA & GLBA)

Last Updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Center Street Lending Corp., Innovate Realty Inc., and Honest Escrow Inc. (collectively "we", "us", or "our") collect, use, share, and protect your information.

3.1 Information We Collect

  • Direct Identifiers: Name, phone number, email address, property addresses, loan identification numbers, corporate entity names (LLC, Corp).
  • Transaction & Property Information: Construction timelines, exit strategies, estimated listing prices, payoff balances, and escrow details.
  • Digital & Analytics Data: IP address, device specifications, browser type, referral representative UTM parameters, and form submission data.

3.2 Cross-Entity Sharing Under Common Ownership

Because Center Street Lending, Innovate Realty, and Honest Escrow operate under a unified corporate umbrella, information submitted through this website or directly to company representatives is shared among these affiliated entities to coordinate your loan payoff, evaluate eligibility for the 1% listing commission, prepare escrow instructions, and provide customer support.

We do not sell personal information to unaffiliated third parties for monetary consideration.

3.3 California Consumer Privacy Rights (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents possess the following statutory rights:

  • Right to Know / Access: Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected, sources, and third parties with whom it is shared.
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of personal data, subject to mandatory record retention laws under California DRE, DFPI, and financial regulatory statutes.
  • Right to Correct: Request correction of inaccurate personal records.
  • Right to Opt-Out: Direct us not to share your data for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will not deny services, charge different prices, or provide a different quality of service for exercising your privacy rights.

To exercise these rights, submit a verifiable request to: privacy@centerstreetlending.com or call (800) 555-5555.

3.4 Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA) Notice

Non-public personal financial information gathered in connection with commercial lending by Center Street Lending Corp. or escrow transactions by Honest Escrow Inc. is safeguarded with administrative, technical, and physical safeguards in accordance with federal and California Financial Information Privacy Act requirements.
